What’s going on

Equations. Momentum balance and Hooke’s law for an isotropic solid, as a first-order system: ρ ∂v/∂t = ∇·σ, ∂σ/∂t = λ (∇·v) I + μ (∇v + ∇vᵀ), in the vertical plane (P-SV). ρ = 1 and λ = μ, so VS = VP/√3 everywhere.

Numerics. Velocity–stress finite differences on a staggered grid (Virieux 1986), 2nd order, on 304 × 156 cells on the CPU (a coarser grid; switch to the GPU solver in Settings for a finer one). Peak frequency 2.7 Hz, 28 grid points per P wavelength at v = 1.

Free surface. The top edge is traction-free: σzz = 0 on it and σxz is mirrored with opposite sign above it (stress imaging). The other edges absorb.

Colours. Cyan is the divergence ∇·v (volume change: P waves), amber the curl ∇×v (rotation: S waves). The seismograms show the ground velocity at the surface: Z positive up, X positive to the right.
