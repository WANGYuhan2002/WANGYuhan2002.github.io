    02 The cable

    1,872 channels · 8.17 m

    One telecom cable between Kefalonia and Ithaca: 15 km of land, sea, land.

    light pulse at ch 00000.00 km of fibre

    Channel number is not position: the fibre to the beach manhole is 7.32 km long, the ground under it 6.44 km. Spare fibre sits in coils along the way. How I map the channels ↗

    06 SeismoHub

    FastAPI · SQLite

    A literature hub for seismology that runs on your own machine.

    It tracks new papers, reads them with AI and anchors each citation to the paragraph it comes from. PDFs are harvested with their licences respected. One process, one database file.
